Character Role Description
Link Hero The protagonist, on a quest to rescue his sister and defeat Ganondorf.
Princess Zelda Princess of Hyrule Assists Link in his quest, disguised as the pirate Tetra.
Ganondorf Antagonist The main antagonist, seeking the power of the Triforce for his own gain.
Tetra Pirate Leader Zelda's alter ego, a feisty and capable pirate captain.
King of Red Lions Talking Boat Link's companion and guide throughout the Great Sea.
Aryll Link's Sister Link's younger sister, kidnapped by Ganondorf at the start of the game.
Medli Rito Sage A Rito girl who serves as the Sage of Earth, aiding Link on his quest.
Makar Korok Sage A Korok musician who becomes the Sage of Wind, assisting Link.
Prince Komali Rito Chieftain The young leader of the Rito tribe, initially unsure of his role.
Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule King of Hyrule The former king of Hyrule, now cursed to wander the Great Sea in spirit form.
